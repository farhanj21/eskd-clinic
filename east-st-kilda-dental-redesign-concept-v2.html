The quiet questions

Things you might be wondering

It's been years since I went. Will you judge me?

Never. A huge number of our patients come to us after a long gap. There are no lectures and no raised eyebrows here — only a warm welcome and a care plan to move forward.

I'm really nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes, and you're in good company. You set the pace, we explain everything before we do it, and you can stop at any moment just by raising a hand.

Do you take my health fund?

We accept all major Australian health funds and claim on the spot, so most of the time there's nothing to pay upfront beyond your gap.

I'm in pain today — can you see me?

Call us on (03) 9527 3678. We keep time aside every day for emergencies and will do everything we can to see you the same day.

Where do I park?

There's off-street parking off Orrong Road. Trams 5 and 64 and bus 220 stop nearby, and Armadale station is a 10–15 minute walk.
